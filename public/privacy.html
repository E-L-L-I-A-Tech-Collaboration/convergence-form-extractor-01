Privacy Policy — Mind Forge

Mind Forge (Convergence Form Extractor) · Effective November 13, 2026

Mind Forge ("the app") is published by Axiom Zeta Innovations. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Accounts

If you create an account, we collect the email address you provide and store your password only as a salted hash — never in plain text. Your profile may hold a display name, your curriculum progress, and your account tier. We use a session cookie to keep you signed in. Accounts are optional: you can use the app's core exercises without one.

2. Subscriptions

If you subscribe through Google Play, your payment is processed by Google under Google Play's terms. We store only your Play Store order ID and subscription status so we can verify your subscription. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

3. AI creativity exercises

When you use the AI creativity exercises, the text you enter is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our server and forwarded to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your response. Google processes this content under its own privacy policy. If you never use the AI exercises, nothing you type is sent to any AI service.

4. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not combine your information with data from other sources.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and its data by contacting us at the developer email address listed on our Google Play store listing. Data stored only on your device can be deleted by clearing the site's stored data in your browser or app settings.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at the developer email address listed on our Google Play store listing.
